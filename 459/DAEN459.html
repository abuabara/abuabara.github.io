DAEN 459
Capstone Senior Design Planning

General Information

  • Description: First in a two-course sequence for the capstone senior design experience; formation of a senior design team, visitation with the team sponsor, preparation of the groundwork for the project, preparation of the project charter and collection or acquisition of initial set of data; provision of instructions on different aspects of capstone design, including ethics, design constraints, applicable standards, project management, report writing specifications and requirements, and oral and visual presentations.

Topics

"If you fail to plan, you plan to fail."

  • Course intro, team formation
  • Interaction with sponsor / site visit
Planning
  • Project planning process, project charter components
  • Preliminary data collection
  • Developing the project charter
  • Risk assessment and mitigation
  • Data and architecture standards
Design & Execution
  • Engineering design, design constraints
Monitoring & Governance
  • Project management principles
  • Ethics

(may change)

Milestones

"A goal without a plan is just a wish." Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

  • Capstone collaboration agreement (S/U)*
  • Initial meeting with sponsors (S/U)*
  • First draft of project charter (graded)
  • Strategic discussion with advisors (S/U)*
  • Second draft of project charter (graded)
  • Preliminary data collection (S/U)*
  • Rehearsal presentations (includes peers and advisors; S/U)*
  • Peer review (S/U)*
  • Final project charter
    • Submission of the charter signed by the sponsor (graded)
    • Project presentation (include advisors and sponsors as conditions allow; graded)

* S: satisfactory/U: unsatisfactory
(subject to adjustments according to each project and team)
