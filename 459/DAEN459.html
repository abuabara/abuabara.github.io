Description

First in a two-course sequence for the capstone senior design experience: formation of a senior design team; visitation with the team sponsor; preparation of project groundwork; creation of the project charter; acquisition of an initial data set. Emphasizes ethics, design constraints, applicable standards, project management, technical writing, oral and visual communication.

Learning Objectives

  1. Translate an open-ended sponsor problem into a concise, data-engineering project charter with measurable goals and constraints.
  2. Conduct sponsor meetings, elicit requirements, and document acceptance criteria.
  3. Evaluate data-centric solutions for privacy, security, bias, and sustainability impacts; apply IEEE/ACM codes of ethics.
  4. Build a work-breakdown structure, Gantt/Agile plans, risk register, and cost estimate for a semester-long technical project.
  5. Identify and apply relevant standards (ISO 25012 for data quality, FAIR principles, SEC data-retention, GDPR, etc.).
  6. Produce a coherent written proposal and deliver a persuasive 10-minute pitch using professional visuals.
