Milestones

"A goal without a plan is just a wish." Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

  • Capstone collaboration agreement (S/U)*
  • Initial meeting with sponsors (S/U)*
  • First draft of project charter (graded)
  • Strategic discussion with advisors (S/U)*
  • Preliminary data collection (S/U)*
  • Second draft of project charter (graded)
  • Advisor review session (S/U)*
  • Rehearsal presentations (includes peers, advisors, and instructors; S/U)*
  • Peer review form (S/U)*
  • Final project charter
    • Submission of the charter signed by the sponsor (graded)
    • Project presentation (include advisors and sponsors as conditions allow; graded)

* S: satisfactory/U: unsatisfactory
(subject to adjustments according to each project and team)
