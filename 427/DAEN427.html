DAEN 427 / ISEN 427
Decision and Risk Analysis

General Information

  • Description: In this course we’ll learn how to model decisions, risk, and preferences using Bayesian inference. We’ll explore how to make choices under uncertainty and how those choices differ from rational models. Drawing from economics, psychology, and management science, we’ll apply these ideas to engineering systems.
