\section*{Goal}
This week, your team will examine your capstone project through the lens of \textbf{ethics, privacy, security, bias, and responsible data use}. The purpose is to identify risks early and propose concrete design or governance decisions that address them.

\section*{Team Task}
Review your current project and prepare a short assessment covering the following areas.

\subsection*{1. Data and privacy}
\begin{itemize}
  \item What data are you collecting, storing, or processing?
  \item Does the project involve personal, sensitive, or potentially identifying data?
  \item Are you collecting more data than you actually need?
  \item What are your plans for access control, retention, and deletion?
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{2. Security}
\begin{itemize}
  \item What are the main security risks in your architecture?
  \item Where are credentials, API keys, or secrets stored?
  \item Who should have access to raw data?
  \item What security controls should be added to your pipeline, database, API, or application?
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{3. Bias and fairness}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Could the data underrepresent or misrepresent particular groups or situations?
  \item What sampling, measurement, historical, or labeling biases might exist?
  \item Could your project's outputs have different consequences for different users or groups?
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{4. Ethics and responsible use}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Who benefits from the system?
  \item Who could be harmed if the system produces incorrect or misleading results?
  \item Are there uses of your system that should be discouraged or explicitly prohibited?
  \item What assumptions should users know about before relying on your results?
\end{itemize}

\section*{Deliverable for Next Friday's Lab}
Prepare a \textbf{3--5 minute team presentation} with approximately \textbf{3--4 slides}:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Project context:} briefly describe your project and the data it uses.
  \item \textbf{Top risks:} identify the 2--4 most important ethical, privacy, security, or bias concerns.
  \item \textbf{Responsible design decisions:} describe specific actions your team will take to reduce these risks.
  \item \textbf{Open question:} end with one issue where your team would like feedback from the class.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{What Makes a Strong Submission}
A strong presentation should move beyond statements such as ``we will protect privacy'' or ``we will avoid bias.'' Connect each concern to a specific part of your project and propose a concrete technical, organizational, or documentation response.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Guiding question:} What could go wrong with our project, and what are we doing about it?
