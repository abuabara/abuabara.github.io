What’s The Most Important Thing?

  • The “most important thing” is subjective.
  • It’s hard to enjoy anything without health, meaningful connections, or a sense of direction.
  • Primary dimensions: Health, Love & Relationships, Purpose & Meaning, Gratitude, Freedom, Learning & Growth, Self-Care.
  • This set of dimensions makes a conceptual backbone for our capstone, because it positions the course not as a technical culminating experience, but as a professional and personal transition course.

The Bridge Between Academic Training and Life (After Graduation)

  • Learn to go beyond working your team’s project and develop the ability to work in any project!
  • Others may always outpace you in coding, surveying, or technical execution.
  • Your true advantage is systems thinking, an advantage that reaches its full power only when paired with strong communication!

ISEN Mission Statement

To serve the state, nation and global community by educating industrial engineering students to be well founded in engineering fundamentals and to have the knowledge and skills required to design, develop, improve, implement and control sophisticated production and service systems in an environment characterized by complex technical and social challenges. Throughout this educational process, students will be instilled with the highest standards of professional and ethical behavior.
